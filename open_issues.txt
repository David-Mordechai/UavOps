Still open

12. (New, minor) Right after "send 998 to the red car and point its payload there", the reply said
    "Orbiting target" while 998 was still flying there (Transiting). The tool calls were right; only
    the wording ran ahead of the UAV. Seen once in the chat run; not measured.

13. (New, note) AoiSearchMissionLiveTests: 8/8 when run alone, 7/8 while six live test classes
    shared the model server - the one miss asked "Which UAV tail number?" in text instead of calling
    PrepareAoiSearch (the load-related pattern already recorded in McpMoav's ToolsConfig.yaml).
    Harmless (the operator answers and it proceeds), but visible under load.
    2026-09-29: the same miss hit 6/8 even alone with the first team-search wording; an empty
    tailNumbers list now means "not named" (the host asks), and it's 8/8 alone again. Not yet
    re-measured under load.

14. (New, 2026-09-28) A typed search target that isn't a word can be turned into a different
    word by the model before it reaches the tools: a ZoneB search went out as "breach" (no voice
    involved; probably a misspelled "bridge"). The detector then searched for exactly that. Not
    changed: the tool passes the operator's own words on purpose.

15. (New, note) Structure searches (anything not a vehicle) were measured on one photo (ZoneB): 2
    pylons, 1 overpass, 10 look-alikes. The second look at a different width is what keeps false
    hits out; a new kind of structure or scene may need its own check. A tall pole seen obliquely
    was sometimes a "utility pole" (not counted as a pylon).

16. (New, 2026-09-29) Live tests leak into a running app. With UavOps.Agent running on :5262,
    a live-test run posted "Lesson emergency-procedures.ps1 completed successfully" into the
    operator's chat twice (seen during the team-search demo). The live tests' McpSimulator
    connects back to whatever host is listening on :5262 (HubConnectionStarter) and posts its
    lesson summary there. McpMoav was already isolated the same way (the live tests launch it with
    --OperationBackend Simulated); McpSimulator needs the equivalent, e.g. no hub connection when
    launched by the tests. Until then: don't run live tests while the app is up.

19. (New, 2026-09-30) Detector recall on top-down vehicles. 2026-10-01: D-FINE-X on 2x2 tiles finds
    every placed vehicle (paused scene, 10 frames each: 40/40, all above the track-start score).
    Still open: small or partly covered vehicles, and real (not placed) traffic. Fine-tuning on
    VisDrone (on the GX10) remains the next step if that's needed.

20. (New, 2026-09-30) Find and track has no live test yet (the model-side part: "search ZoneA for a
    red car and track it" -> PrepareAoiSearch track=true, measured once in chat). "Stop tracking"

Done 2026-10-01
---------------
- 21: "stop tracking" asked "Which UAV do you mean?" with only 998 tracking. McpMoav now tells the
  host (generic SetOperatorUav) that a UAV which locked on the target is the operator's current UAV;
  a model guess of exactly that UAV is grounded. Chrome: "stop tracking" -> 998 stopped, no question.
- 22 and 18: ~3 fps per UAV with two searching. The GPU clock was never the limit (306 MHz was the
  idle reading; under load it was 98% busy at a raised clock); pinned at max it ran no faster and
  warmer idle, so pinning was undone. D-FINE-S would be 3.2x faster but lost the white vehicles
  (track-start 2/10 van, 0/10 white car vs X 10/10). X stays: the car was found on the first pass
  in both Chrome runs. Device under the search: ~73 C, 19.5 W (throttles ~99 C).
- Mission step messages are short and name the UAVs ("Step 3 (998 found the red car): 997
  returning home."; the voice says only "997 returning home."). Voice plays at 1.25x. The map's
  target ring and the followed detection's marker move with the car (they trailed it by up to
  ~1.5 s / 50 m). Chat font 1.5x the base (was 2x).
- Spotting the car: the onboard detector runs on 2x2 tiles of the full frame (the red car scores
  0.6-0.74, was 0.24 - under the 0.35 that starts a track); searches fly at 70 kts (set by
  PrepareAoiSearch with SetSpeed; was 105); the map shows every airborne UAV's camera footprint, line
  of sight and tail number, and warns when sped up while onboard detection runs. Chrome at 1x, the
  operator's plan sentence (997+998, ZoneA, red car, track; when found return the other home): 998
  found the car on the FIRST pass (~70 s after start; before: 16 passes at 5x), 74%, locked and
  followed it within 2-12 m of its true position; 997 went home and landed; "stop tracking" ended it.
- Mission plans ("...when the car is found, return the other UAV home"): MissionPlanLiveTests 8/8.
